ASIAWORKSHandover · At a glance

A directory built for AsiaWorks graduates.

AsiaWorks Direktori is where graduates list their businesses, the public finds them, and graduates find each other to work together. It is live, in Bahasa Indonesia, and works the same on a phone and on a desktop.

  • The staff console at /staff, where your team verifies graduates and runs the directory.
  • The full source code, in a GitHub repository, with setup instructions.

ASIAWORKSHandover · Features

What each person can do.

There are four kinds of people on the site. Each one sees and does more than the one before.

Visitor · no account
  • Search by keyword, category and area (Jabodetabek, Makassar, Luar Jabodetabek, or any other region).
  • See businesses on a map or as a list, and open any business page.
  • Browse Peluang, events, GLP Promo and collaboration stories.
  • Tap an owner's name to see "Profil lulusan": name, programs and batch.
Member · signed up
  • Sign up in three steps and choose the programs finished (IB, IA, LP). LP needs its batch number.
  • Send a Hubungkan request to any business, or answer a Peluang.
  • RSVP to events, with a waiting list when an event is full.
  • Save businesses to come back to, and report a listing to staff.
Graduate · verified by staff
  • List one or more businesses. Several branches are one listing with several locations.
  • Put a pin on the map, show only an approximate area, or mark the business "Online saja".
  • Post Peluang, also without a business listing, and offer a GLP Promo to fellow graduates.
  • Open a business's contact through "Lihat kontak". The owner sees who looked.
  • Offer to host an event in their city.
Business owner · in the dashboard
  • Edit the listing at any time. Changes go live at once.
  • Switch a listing off and on. Off hides it everywhere and keeps the content.
  • See how many people viewed it, opened its contact and asked to connect.
  • Accept or decline Hubungkan requests. Accepting opens both sides' contacts.
  • Delete a listing or the whole account, for good.

How graduates work together

HubungkanA short request from one member to a business. Only the owner answers it. Staff cannot read these.
PeluangA real need, posted publicly: a supplier, partner, vendor, consultant, freelancer or hire.
GLP PromoA deal a business offers to fellow graduates, shown on its card. The owner can switch it off.
AcaraEvents with RSVP, a seat limit and a waiting list.
CeritaA collaboration story between two businesses, written by staff and published only when both owners agree.

Staff sign in at /staff. The first screen shows what is waiting for a decision, and a red badge on each section counts its queue. On a phone the sections sit in a bar at the bottom.

A normal week for staff

  • Verifikasi: check new sign-ups against the batch records and approve or reject them.
  • Moderasi: review new businesses, Peluang and Promo, and answer reports.
  • Banner: keep the slider current. A picture of 1200 × 400 fits best, with its message inside the picture.
  • Acara: add upcoming events so the page is never empty.
  • Privasi: act on deletion requests from here, not from the Supabase dashboard. Only this route also removes the person's stored files.

Check the four Auto Approve switches

The console has four switches made for demos: Auto Approve Graduates Request, Auto Approve Bisnis, Auto Approve Peluang and Auto Approve Promo. While one is on, whatever is sent goes live with no review.

For normal running, keep all four off. They are in Verifikasi and Moderasi, and every automatic approval is written to the audit log.

ASIAWORKSHandover · Rules and privacy

The rules that protect members.

These rules live inside the database, not in the screens. Someone who bypasses the website and talks to the database directly meets the same rules.

Publishing needs two yeses

A business appears only with the owner's consent and a moderator's approval, and only while its owner is a verified graduate.

Graduate status comes from staff

Never from what a person types. Programs and batch numbers lock once the request is sent. Withdrawing the status takes that person's listings down.

Contacts are private

Phone numbers and business contacts are stored encrypted. They reach another person only through "Lihat kontak" (verified graduates, and it is recorded) or an accepted Hubungkan request.

A suspended listing vanishes everywhere

Search, the map, Peluang, promos, stories and ads all drop it at the same moment. The same goes for a listing its owner switched off.

Location can be approximate

A home business can show an area instead of a point. The database rounds the point to about 1 km before storing it, so the exact spot is never kept.

Deletion is real

Deleting a listing or an account removes the data and the stored pictures. Only a payment's amount and date would remain, detached from the person.

Banners and ads belong to staff

Members cannot add, change or remove one. An ad carries the label "Iklan" and leaves the home page while its business is not public.

Staff actions are logged

Approvals, rejections, suspensions and deletions are written to the audit log by the database itself.

ASIAWORKSHandover · Technology and access

How it is built, and where it lives.

The directory is deliberately small: one web application and one database service. There is no server of our own to patch or restart.

PartWhat is usedWhat it does
WebsiteNext.js 16, React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS 4All nine pages. The whole feature is one folder, app/(aw), so it can be moved into another Next.js site.
HostingVercelBuilds and publishes the site every time the main branch changes.
Database and loginSupabase (PostgreSQL)Accounts, all directory data, the permission rules, search, and picture storage.
MapLeaflet with OpenStreetMapThe map and its pins. No API key and no usage fee.
Source codeGitHubRepository insurgent6a-pixel/AWDirektori. Each change arrives as a pull request.
Domaindirektori.asiaworks.idA subdomain of asiaworks.id pointing at Vercel.

How a change reaches the live site

  • The change is made on its own branch and opened as a pull request on GitHub.
  • Someone with rights approves and merges it into main.
  • Vercel builds main and the live site updates within a few minutes.
  • A change to the database comes as a dated file in supabase/upgrades/. It is pasted into Supabase's SQL Editor, oldest first. Each file is safe to run twice and deletes nothing.

The site's two Supabase settings are stored in Vercel and are built into the site, so changing one needs a redeploy.

Access handover checklist

Tick each one when AsiaWorks holds an owner-level login.

  • GitHub: the repository, with a named AsiaWorks owner. It is public today; decide whether to make it private.
  • Vercel: the project and its environment variables.
  • Supabase: the project, including the service role key, kept private.
  • Domain: the DNS record for direktori.asiaworks.id.
  • Staff console: at least two staff accounts, each a named person.
  • Supabase plan: confirmed, with backups (see page 9).
  • All database upgrade files confirmed as run on the live project.

Adding a staff account

The person signs up at /daftar (the first step is enough). Someone with Supabase access then sets that account's role to staff in the SQL Editor; the exact line is in the README. After that they sign in at /staff.

ASIAWORKSHandover · Limits

What it is sized for, and what it leaves out.

The brief asked for a light, simple build for 35 to 100 members. These are the choices that kept it light. None of them is a problem today; each has a clear upgrade when the directory grows.

Choice madeWhat it means todayWhen to revisit

Peluang and Acara filter in the browserOne fetch, then filtering with no waiting.Past a few hundred Peluang or events.
Page views are a simple counterCounted once per browser session. Good for a rough picture, not for billing.If view numbers are ever sold to advertisers.
Audit log shows the latest 500 actionsOlder actions stay stored; the screen just does not list them.When staff need to look further back.

Deleted pictures linger brieflyA deleted picture is gone from storage at once, but its direct address can answer for up to a minute.No action needed.

Not included in this build

Online payments

No payment provider is connected and no screen asks for money. Staff put ads up by hand. The database is ready for payments, so connecting a provider later does not need a rebuild.

Email sending at volume

Supabase's built-in mailer sends only a few messages an hour. For password resets to work reliably as membership grows, connect AsiaWorks' own email service (SMTP) in Supabase.

Notifications

Members see new Hubungkan requests as a badge in their dashboard. The site does not send email or WhatsApp alerts.

A member's own data export

Members can ask for their account to be deleted from the dashboard. A self-service copy of their data is not offered.

ASIAWORKSHandover · Maintenance

Keeping it healthy.

Most of the work is the staff's weekly routine on page 5. The technical upkeep below is small, but skipping it is how a site quietly ages.

How oftenTaskWhy it matters
WeeklyClear the Verifikasi and Moderasi queues. Open the live site and try one search.Graduates wait on staff to go live.
MonthlyCheck the Supabase dashboard: database size, storage used, and that a recent backup exists.Pictures and data grow with every listing.
MonthlyRead the audit log for anything unexpected, and confirm the four Auto Approve switches are off.Catches a switch left on after a demo.
QuarterlyUpdate the site's software packages (Next.js, React, Supabase client), then run the build and npm run check.Security fixes arrive as package updates.
QuarterlyReview staff accounts. Remove the role from people who left the team.Staff can see every account and contact.
YearlyConfirm the domain and all service accounts are still owned by a current AsiaWorks person.Logins tied to one person are the usual way a site is lost.
After any changeRun npm run devlog so the dev log sheet stays complete.One place to see what changed and when.

Decide the Supabase plan now

At the time of writing, a Supabase project on the free plan is paused after about a week with no activity and has no automatic backups. For a site holding members' details, confirm the plan and make sure daily backups are on.

If something looks wrong

  • "Data belum bisa dimuat": the site cannot reach Supabase. Check that the project is running.
  • A change is not showing: check the latest deployment in Vercel.
  • A business is missing: look it up in Bisnis in the console. It states the reason.

Running costs to expect

There is no licence fee for the directory itself, and the map is free. The recurring costs are the Vercel and Supabase plans and the asiaworks.id domain. Which plans fit depends on traffic and on the backup decision above, so they are best confirmed together at handover.

ASIAWORKSHandover · Maintenance options

Four ways to look after it.

The directory is yours either way. The options differ in who does the technical upkeep on page 9 and who is on hand when something needs changing.

Option A

Self-managed

For a team with its own developer who knows React and SQL.

  • AsiaWorks holds every login and does all upkeep.
  • This document, the README and the dev log are the guide.
  • One handover session to walk through the code and console.

No ongoing fee. Help afterwards is quoted per request.

Option B

On call

For a stable site that needs a hand only now and then.

  • Fixes and small changes whenever you ask.
  • Each job quoted before work starts.
  • Routine upkeep stays with AsiaWorks.

Pay per job. No guaranteed response time.

Option C · suggested for the first three months

Care plan

For peace of mind while the directory fills up after launch.

  • All the monthly and quarterly upkeep on page 9, done for you.
  • Backups checked, packages updated, rules re-tested.
  • A set amount of small changes each month.
  • Priority response when something breaks.
  • A short monthly note: what was done, how the directory grew.

Fixed monthly fee, to be agreed.

Option D

Care and growth

For a directory AsiaWorks wants to keep building on.

  • Everything in the Care plan.
  • A planned feature each cycle, chosen together from page 11.
  • Staff training when the console changes.
  • A quarterly review of usage and what members ask for.

Monthly fee plus an agreed feature budget.

The dev log

Every change since the first build is listed in docs/devlog.csv, which opens in Excel or Google Sheets. Each row has the date, the part of the site, what changed and why, whether it is live, and a reference to the pull request and the exact code change.

The sheet is written from the project's own history, so it cannot drift from what was really done. After new work, npm run devlog brings it up to date.
